## The question

Can simple local rules make a graph grow a real dimension, like space; which keep one they are given; and can the
null map predict a law before it runs (the operator's words, 2026-10-13)? A law is a few energy terms and how the graph
may change; each is grown blindly from eight starts (empty, random, six lattices), 64 to a million nodes, mean degree
to 32 in the pilots.

## Words you will see

**law**: energy terms and dynamics. **rung**: one graph size (N). **cap**: the mean degree above which a graph is not
studied. **d_s**: the dimension a random walk feels. **start**: what a law is grown from: empty, random, square,
triangular, cubic, bcc, fcc, tesseract (4D). **chain**: a law's run from one start, the Markov chain of graphs it grows. **twin starts**: empty and random. **budget**: a seed's work, as the engines count it, the same on any machine.
**let-through**: the share of the laws a gate would withhold that goes one size on, so the gate is checked.
