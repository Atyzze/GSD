Map

The study's best laws, one at a time, inside its own folder: each law's zone (its neighbours, its seeds, each term switched off). The fleet is in one of three modes, never mixed: the N-push while the Top page's push list holds a law, else the zone search while a zone has work, else the blind ladder; within a mode it takes its work in one order, runs and probes alike: the zone of the law in focus, the Dex laws read again, the blind draw, earlier laws' leftovers. A zone stands when two thirds of its ring at 0.1 read the law's kind, or else of a smaller ring from 0.01; it is then mapped by rings outward or by lines halved toward each neighbour of another kind; every mapped zone is named Z1, Z2 and so on, each coupling's range drawn as its laws are read, a term a law lacks counted as 0. Changes need the admin link; a setting applies from the next law, so a reading under way never changes under its feet.

Why this page exists

Every decision this runtime makes flows from four probes (d_s, d_B, d_w, d_V). Before the fleet collects a single sample, the probes are run against graphs whose dimensions are known by construction, every family to the top of its ladder at ten seeds per cell, in the foreground, with no time limit. This page is the interpretation of that table: what was measured, what theory says, how far apart they are, whether the gap is acceptable and why, and where more N buys accuracy and where it demonstrably does not. Nothing is re-derived in the browser; it is the calibration table (calibration/probe_calibration.json), served as written.

Deviation per family, rung and probe

Every probe against finite-size ladders of graphs whose dimensions are exact by construction (1D/2D/3D tori, the Sierpinski gasket) plus a refusal anchor (a random 4-regular expander, a different draw per seed, which has no finite dimension of any kind). Each cell shows the mean over seeds, the spread across seeds, the deviation from theory, and the tolerance band drawn with the measured value marked. A confident reading on the expander is a defect, and it misreads the campaign's no-scaling-regime graphs the same way. The structure columns stop where the census does, by design: a rung above the size a part is measured at says "not measured", with the size on hover, rather than leaving a dash that reads as lost data. The rows above a family's frozen ladder (to about 4 M nodes) are measured with the same probes and census and gate nothing: not the startup gate, a verdict, a trend or a fit.

ESS vs sweep budget

The same engine that runs campaigns, on a fixed reference Hamiltonian, across a ramp of sweep budgets at each N, read back by the certification screen's own estimator. Where the median ESS clears the floor is the measured sweep requirement per rung; S0 should come from this table, not from a guess. Cells marked unresolved have an autocorrelation time finer than the recording grid: a chain too short to measure its own correlation reports an ESS that flatters it, and such cells never satisfy the floor.

Workers

How many executor slots run at once, changed while the run goes on. A lower count stops the slots above it now, without waiting for their rungs: each slot saves its seed where it is and gives the rung back to the queue, a slot that stays resumes it from the save, and nothing is charged to a law. A higher count starts slots at once. The ramp hands the count back to the run's own rule.
